Common questions

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The pilot is free, and we will not ask for payment details until you decide to continue.

Who approves my account?

We do. Each sign-up is reviewed by a person before an agent is set up, so you start with someone on our side who knows your business.

What can the agent change?

Only what you approve. It shows you each step first, saves email as drafts and never sends, and only adds a calendar event after your yes. Read the details in our Privacy Policy.

What is a credit?

A credit is how we measure usage. A normal chat message costs 2 credits. An action, such as drafting an email or adding a calendar event, costs 5. Your dashboard shows how many you have left.

What happens when I run out?

Your agent pauses until your credits renew, or you can add 500 credits for $10 at any time. We will never charge you without asking first.

What are the fair-use limits?

To keep the service reliable we cap some heavy features, such as AI-written code changes, per day. Normal day-to-day use of chat, calendar and drafting is what the pilot is built for.
