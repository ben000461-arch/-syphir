What it is

An assistant that works for your business, safely.

Your agent takes the routine work off your plate and keeps a record of everything it does. It is built to be trusted: it prepares, you decide.

// automate

Everyday work, handled

Email drafts, calendar questions, spreadsheets, notes and small code changes. Ask in plain words, the way you would text a coworker.

// approval

It asks before it acts

Anything that changes something is shown to you first. It saves drafts, it never sends your email, and it only reads your calendar.

// logs

Every action on record

See what your agent did, when, and how it went. Logs keep counts and names only, never the text of your emails or files.

// isolation

Your agent, only yours

Each business gets its own agent and its own workspace. Nobody else can see your chat, your files or your logs.
